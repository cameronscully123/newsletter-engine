Questions we get on every call

Will it actually sound like me?

Every issue starts from your own words in the Loom, so the ideas, stories and opinions are yours. We shape them into an email and match your tone from your past content. And you don't pay unless you approve the first issue.

How much of my time does this take?

About five minutes to record a Loom and a few minutes to approve the draft. Many clients stop reviewing after the first month and let us run it.

I don't have a big list. Is it worth it?

A small list of the right people beats a big list of strangers. We start with the contacts you already have (past leads, clients, call no-shows) and grow from there with your lead magnet and referrals.

Which email platform do you use?

We build on Beehiiv, where we're certified experts. If you're on another platform, we handle the move for you, including your contacts and sign-up forms.

When will I see booked calls?

Replies usually start within the first few issues. Booked calls build as your list gets to know you, which is why we start with a 3-month term and set targets on day one.

What happens after the first 3 months?

You go month to month and can cancel with 30 days' notice. Your list, your content and your platform account are always yours.

Can you run ads to grow my list?

Yes. Paid subscriber growth is available as an add-on once your newsletter is running. Ad spend is paid directly by you.
